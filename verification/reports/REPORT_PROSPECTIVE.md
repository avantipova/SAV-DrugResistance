# Superseded

This report's framing was wrong: it described seven resistance substitutions as "reported after
the training freeze", but six of the seven were first reported in 2009, 2014, 2019 or 2024 — the
harvest's date filter constrained the search window, not when each variant was first described.
It also scored three variants against models for drugs those variants are not reported resistant
to.

Corrected and replaced by **`REPORT_LABEL_COVERAGE.md`**, which states what the experiment
actually measures (label coverage, not prospective validation), audits the drug attribution per
variant, and reports 1 of 3 on the variants with sound evidence and 0 of 1 on the single
genuinely new one.
